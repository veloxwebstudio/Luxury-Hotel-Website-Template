Our story

Made by the coast

Aurelia Bay began with a simple idea: create a place that feels more like a beautiful house than a conventional hotel.

Our philosophy

Luxury here is measured in time, light and how welcome you feel.

The resort is intentionally small: 34 keys, one restaurant, a generous pool and a spa designed around local botanicals and slow rituals. Materials are tactile and quiet; service is present without being intrusive.

A coastal plot
